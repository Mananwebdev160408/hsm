PART 1: THE OPENING HOOK & THE PROBLEM (0:00 – 0:45)
================================================================================
[Action on Screen: Start on Homepage http://localhost:3000 at the top]

Speaker Says:

"Good morning / afternoon everyone.

Let me ask you a simple question: Where is the purchase invoice for your 
smartphone or laptop right now?

For most of us in India, invoices are scattered across email threads, faded 
thermal receipts in a drawer, or lost completely. When our devices break down, 
we don't even know if our warranty has expired. Worse, when we buy insurance—
whether health, vehicle, or travel—we buy blindly from aggregators that push 
sponsored rankings without understanding what we actually own.

Today, I'm excited to present BillBazaar.

BillBazaar is India's first unified platform that bridges the gap between 
valuable personal assets and informed insurance decisions. It connects a 
Digital Asset Locker for your invoices and warranties with an objective, 
side-by-side policy comparison engine across Health, Motor, Life, and Travel 
insurance."

PART 4: SCREEN 4 — THE SIDE-BY-SIDE COMPARISON MATRIX (2:30 – 3:30)
================================================================================
[Action on Screen: Click 'Compare Now' on the floating dock to go to /compare]

Speaker Says:

"This screen represents one of the most critical experiences in BillBazaar: 
the Side-by-Side Policy Comparison Matrix.

Traditional aggregators make comparing policies difficult because terms are 
buried in 40-page PDF policy wordings. BillBazaar standardizes every critical 
policy parameter into a clean, objective matrix:

1. Financial Specs: We compare Annual Premiums side-by-side, along with Sum 
   Insured and compulsory copayment percentages.
2. Hospitalisation Limits: Notice row 2—we explicitly highlight room rent 
   restrictions. Aarogya Kavach covers Single Private AC rooms, while Suraksha 
   360 covers any room including suites.
3. Waiting Periods: We clearly display the pre-existing disease cooling-off 
   periods (2 years vs. 1 year express rider).
4. Inclusions & Exclusions: We place inclusions and explicit policy exclusions 
   side-by-side so the customer is never blindsided during a claim.
5. IRDAI Track Record: Verified Claim Settlement Ratios—98.6% versus 99.1%—and 
   customer satisfaction metrics.

Crucially, BillBazaar never marks one plan as universally best. We empower 
consumers to weigh cost versus benefits according to their family's financial 
priorities."

PART 9: Q&A DEFENSE SHEET (FOR JUDGES & EVALUATORS)
================================================================================

Q1: "How does BillBazaar monetize?"
A1: "BillBazaar operates on a dual monetization model: 
     (1) IRDAI-compliant distribution/brokerage commissions when users compare 
         and purchase policies, and 
     (2) OEM and extended warranty affiliate partnerships (e.g., AppleCare, ResQ, 
         Onsitego) when users renew expiring warranties."

Q2: "Why would a user store invoices here instead of Google Drive or Digilocker?"
A2: "Google Drive and Digilocker are passive, unstructured storage folders. They 
     cannot parse invoice dates, cannot compute warranty countdowns, cannot notify 
     you 30 days before expiration, and cannot cross-reference your assets with 
     compatible insurance policies. BillBazaar makes asset data actionable."

Q3: "How do you handle OCR and data privacy?"
A3: "Our Smart Optical Engine processes document metadata into encrypted client-side 
     vaults with AES-256 standards so sensitive financial bills remain secure and 
     private."

Q4: "What is your customer acquisition strategy?"
A4: "We acquire users at the point of sale through retailer integrations (Croma, 
     Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales) when customers receive their digital invoices, 
     bringing them naturally into the BillBazaar ecosystem."
